# Squish & Sprinkle — starter store

Open `index.html` in a browser to preview the storefront.

Included:
- Responsive pastel storefront
- Product cards
- Add-to-cart functionality
- Quantity controls
- Cart saved in the browser
- Checkout form (demo only)

IMPORTANT BEFORE SELLING:
This starter site does NOT process real payments. Do not collect or store card numbers yourself.
An adult should connect a reputable payment/commerce provider and configure shipping, taxes, refunds, privacy/terms, and order notifications before the store accepts real orders.
Replace the sample products and emoji artwork with your real squishy photos and accurate descriptions.
